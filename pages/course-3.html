Course Description

This is the most comprehensive Physics course on the platform, built to cover everything that appears on the Third Year final exam. We move from electromagnetism through energy and circuits to modern physics, with every concept demonstrated visually before you ever see a question on it. The final module is entirely past papers, so you walk in having already seen the exam in every way but the date.

Requirements

  • Second Year Mechanics fundamentals
  • A scientific calculator
  • Comfort with basic vector and trigonometry skills
